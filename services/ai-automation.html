Frequently Asked Questions

Questions About AI & Business Automation

What is an AI Receptionist and how does it integrate with our phones?
An AI Receptionist handles incoming business telephone inquiries 24/7 with human-like conversational intelligence: answering client questions, taking detailed messages, qualifying leads, and booking appointments directly into your calendar or CRM without missing critical business calls.
How does Power Automate eliminate manual data entry?
Power Automate connects disparate software systems (e.g. accounting, ERP, email, CRM). When a document or invoice arrives, the automation extracts key fields via OCR, validates amounts, routes approvals, and updates your ledger without human keystrokes.
Is our corporate data kept private when using AI solutions?
Yes. We engineer enterprise AI deployments using private Azure OpenAI tenants and Microsoft Copilot Studio with strict tenant boundaries. Your business data is never used to train public foundational models.
