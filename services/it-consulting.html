Frequently Asked Questions

Questions About IT Consulting & Architecture

What is the difference between an IT consultant and an MSP?
An MSP focuses on day-to-day helpdesk tickets and packaged recurring subscriptions. As an IT engineering and architecture consultancy, AusNet IT focuses on high-level strategic planning, vendor-independent design, systems audit, and delivering high-stakes technical transformation projects.
How does a Virtual CIO (vCIO) assist mid-market companies?
A Virtual CIO provides senior executive technology leadership without the full-time executive salary overhead: leading board technology presentations, driving IT budgeting, mitigating technical debt, and evaluating vendor proposals.
Can you audit an IT environment before an M&A transaction?
Yes. We perform pre-acquisition IT due diligence: uncovering hidden software licensing liabilities, cybersecurity vulnerabilities, legacy architecture debt, and estimating accurate post-merger integration costs.
Do you provide dedicated technical talent and team augmentation for projects?
Yes. Through our established technology centre in Sri Lanka, AusNet IT provides dedicated project engineering talent and technical staff augmentation. Australian organisations gain access to vetted, senior-level cloud, systems, and software engineering capabilities managed under Australian architecture standards and local accountability.
