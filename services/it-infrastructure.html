Frequently Asked Questions

Questions About IT Infrastructure Engineering

How do you help with Broadcom/VMware licensing cost increases?
Following VMware licence restructuring, we conduct independent hypervisor evaluations: assisting Australian clients with right-sizing VMware footprints, migrating to Microsoft Hyper-V / Azure Stack HCI, or transitioning workloads into Azure.
What is the 3-2-1 backup strategy and how do you protect against ransomware?
We implement a hardened 3-2-1-1-0 architecture: 3 copies of data, on 2 different media, 1 offsite in the cloud, 1 immutable or air-gapped copy that cannot be deleted or encrypted by ransomware, and 0 errors verified via automated recovery testing.
Can you design multi-site redundant SD-WAN networks?
Yes. We design and deploy enterprise SD-WAN topologies with automated carrier failover (e.g. primary fibre, secondary NBN/5G backup), prioritised VoIP/video QoS, and encrypted IPsec tunnels.
