Frequently Asked Questions

Questions About Cybersecurity Consulting

What is ACSC Essential Eight and does my business need it?
The Australian Cyber Security Centre (ACSC) Essential Eight is the baseline mitigation standard for Australian organisations. It covers application control, patch management, restricting admin privileges, MFA, and immutable backups to defend against 85%+ of targeted ransomware and cyber attacks.
How does Privileged Access Management (PIM) protect our servers?
PIM ensures that administrators do not have permanent standing super-user rights. Admin roles are granted Just-in-Time (JIT) for approved time windows with mandatory MFA and audit logging, eliminating lateral movement during a credential breach.
How quickly can you perform a cybersecurity audit?
A standard Essential Eight baseline and Active Directory/Entra ID gap assessment takes between 3 to 7 business days, culminating in a prioritised remediation roadmap.
