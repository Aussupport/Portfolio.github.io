About AusNet IT Solutions

Technology Expertise That Makes a Difference.

AusNet IT Solutions is an Australian IT engineering and consulting business helping organisations navigate complex technology environments.

We focus on delivering practical solutions across cloud infrastructure, Microsoft technologies, cybersecurity and enterprise IT modernisation. Our approach combines technical expertise with an understanding of business requirements, ensuring technology investments deliver meaningful outcomes.

Identity & Positioning

Who We Are

AusNet IT Solutions is a specialist IT engineering and consulting practice based in Melbourne, Victoria, delivering enterprise technology expertise to organisations across Australia and New Zealand.

We operate strictly as an engineering and architecture partner. We do not sell generic computer repairs or basic managed helpdesks; instead, we design, deploy, and govern high-performance cloud environments, resilient infrastructure, and Zero Trust security controls that support core business goals.
